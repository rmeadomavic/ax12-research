## For Drone Friends / FPV Community

*Tone: Excited, community-oriented -- "look what we found"*

1. **DOOM on Gimbals**
   - **What to say:** "The AX12 runs DOOM. With the gimbals. Right on the transmitter screen."
   - **Wow moment:** Hand them the radio, let them strafe with the sticks.
   - *Key phrase:* "It plays DOOM with gimbals."

2. **FM Radio -- Hidden Hardware**
   - **What to say:** "Nobody knew this was in here. There is a fully functional FM radio chip. Plug in headphones as the antenna and you have got broadcast FM."
   - **Wow moment:** Tune to a local station live. Audio comes out the headphone jack.
   - *Key phrase:* "Nobody knew this was in here."

3. **GPS Module Discovery**
   - **What to say:** "There is a GPS receiver in the AX12 that RadioMaster never documented. We can pull live coordinates and generate a Google Maps link from the transmitter."
   - **Wow moment:** Show their current position as a Maps link, generated from the radio.
   - *Key phrase:* "Nobody knew this was in here."

4. **USB Gamepad for Sims**
   - **What to say:** "Flip a switch and the AX12 becomes a USB gamepad. Use your actual transmitter with DRL Simulator, Liftoff, VelociDrone -- any sim."
   - **Wow moment:** Plug USB into a laptop, show the radio appearing as a game controller.
   - *Key phrase:* "Your real radio on your real sim."

5. **Lua Scripting Engine**
   - **What to say:** "The EdgeTX side has full Lua scripting. We have written race timers, telemetry dashboards, and custom widgets. Totally extensible."
   - **Wow moment:** Run a custom Lua script that animates on the OSD.
   - *Key phrase:* "First open-source AX12 toolkit."

6. **UMBUS Protocol Decoded**
   - **What to say:** "We reverse-engineered the serial protocol between the Android brain and the EdgeTX radio core. We call it UMBUS. Every gimbal, switch, pot, and button is mapped."
   - **Wow moment:** Show live UMBUS packet stream with decoded channel values.
   - *Key phrase:* "First open-source AX12 toolkit."

7. **Open-Source Toolkit -- First Mover**
   - **What to say:** "We published the first open-source AX12 research toolkit on GitHub. Tools for model backup, calibration, UMBUS, GPS, FM -- all of it."
   - **Wow moment:** Pull up the GitHub repo and the star count.
   - *Key phrase:* "First open-source AX12 toolkit."

8. **Web Calibrator Dashboard**
   - **What to say:** "There is a live web dashboard running on the transmitter showing real-time gimbal, switch, and pot values. Open it from any device on the same network."
   - **Wow moment:** Open browser on phone, move gimbals, see values update instantly.
   - *Key phrase:* "Look what we found."

## For Students (Active Duty SF / 3rd SFG)

*Tone: Operational, mission-relevant -- "this enhances your capability"*

1. **ATAK/CoT Bridge -- Transmitter as TAK Node**
   - **What to say:** "Your transmitter now generates Cursor-on-Target messages. Every ATAK user on your net sees your pilot position and drone telemetry without any extra hardware."
   - **Wow moment:** Show pilot icon appearing on ATAK map from the transmitter alone.
   - *Key phrase:* "Your transmitter is now a tactical node."

2. **GPS Pilot Tracking**
   - **What to say:** "The AX12 has an onboard GPS. We use it to log and broadcast pilot position. Your TOC knows where you are setting up without you calling it in."
   - **Wow moment:** Display live coordinates and a Google Maps link generated on the radio.
   - *Key phrase:* "Pilot position on the map."

3. **Payload Drop Calculator**
   - **What to say:** "Enter wind speed, altitude, and airspeed. The calculator gives you a release point. No more guessing."
   - **Wow moment:** Show calculated release point overlaid on a map.
   - *Key phrase:* "Zero additional equipment."

4. **TAK OSD Display**
   - **What to say:** "Friendly positions from your ATAK network can be overlaid right on the transmitter screen. Situational awareness without looking away from the radio."
   - **Wow moment:** Show ATAK node labels rendered on the AX12 display.
   - *Key phrase:* "Zero additional equipment."

5. **Meshtastic Mesh Networking**
   - **What to say:** "The AX12 runs the Meshtastic app. Pair it with a LoRa node and your transmitter is on the mesh. Text, GPS, telemetry -- off-grid comms from the radio."
   - **Wow moment:** Send a Meshtastic message from the AX12 to another node.
   - *Key phrase:* "Your transmitter is now a tactical node."

6. **Hydra / Argus Integration**
   - **What to say:** "The Hydra detection payload on the drone talks back to the AX12. Target classifications, bounding boxes, and alerts -- straight to your radio."
   - **Wow moment:** Show a detection alert from Hydra appearing on the transmitter.
   - *Key phrase:* "This enhances your capability."

7. **USB Gamepad for Sim Training**
   - **What to say:** "Before you fly the real thing, plug the AX12 into a laptop via USB. It becomes a game controller. Train on DRL Sim or VelociDrone with the exact radio you will use downrange."
   - **Wow moment:** Plug in, launch sim, fly with AX12 sticks.
   - *Key phrase:* "Zero additional equipment."

8. **Model Backup and Restore**
   - **What to say:** "We can back up every model and template on the radio over WiFi. Lose a radio? Flash a new one and restore your configs in 60 seconds."
   - **Wow moment:** Run a backup, show the JSON files, explain restore.
   - *Key phrase:* "Zero additional equipment."
